# Cute Isometric 3D Game — Kitten Village

A web-based, Hello Kitty Island Adventure–style pastel game: you play a cute kitten, walk around a warm pastel island (WASD/arrows), jump (spacebar), and press E near animal friends to hear them chat. Procedural art only — no external models or textures.

## Tech
- **Vite + TypeScript + Three.js** (`three`, dev: `@types/three`, `vite`, `typescript`)
- Fullscreen canvas, resize handling, 60fps game loop with delta-time
- All art from primitives (spheres, capsules, cylinders, rounded boxes) with soft matte materials

## Look & Feel (matched to your screenshot)
- **Camera**: perspective w/ narrow FOV (~35°) at a high three-quarter angle (~52° pitch, slight yaw) that reads nearly isometric — like the screenshot — smoothly following the kitten with slight look-ahead
- **Palette** (from screenshot analysis): sandy ground `#D89A7A`, grass `#7CBF4E`/`#5E9E3C`, terracotta planks `#C97B54`/`#A05A3E`, pink cobbles `#DDBAC8`, log edging `#C99A5E`, pink/purple hut `#E86CA0`/`#B060C0` w/ yellow awning `#F2C14E`, cream-peach sky `#FFE8D6` + matching fog
- **Lighting**: warm hemisphere ambient + soft directional key light, PCFSoft shadows, sRGB output with no tone mapping so pastels stay true
- **Shapes**: everything chunky, rounded, toy-like — big heads, stubby limbs, lobed tree canopies, scalloped awnings

## Mechanics
- **Move**: WASD/arrows, directions rotated to camera yaw, ~4.5 u/s with accel smoothing; character rotates toward movement
- **Jump**: spacebar, gravity ~24, hop ~1.2 units; stretch going up, squash on landing
- **Collision**: radial island bound + circle colliders on trees/hut/rocks/props (simple push-out)
- **Talk**: nearest NPC within ~2.2 units on E → speech bubble with a cute line; small "E" hint when in range
